Taj Mahal

Agra, Uttar Pradesh

Built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal in 1632.

Nearest airport: Agra Airport. Train: Agra Cantt Station.

Goa

North & South Goa

Famous for beaches, churches and Portuguese heritage.

Manali

Himachal Pradesh

Nearest airport: Bhuntar Airport. Bus from Delhi available.

Jaipur

Rajasthan

Known as Pink City, famous for forts and palaces.

Nearest airport: Jaipur International Airport. Train available.
