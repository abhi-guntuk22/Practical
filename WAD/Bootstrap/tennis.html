Basic Rules of Tennis

  • A tennis match is played between two or four players.
  • Players use rackets to hit the ball over the net.
  • A player must win at least 6 games to win a set.
  • Matches are usually played in best of 3 or 5 sets.
  • The ball must land inside the court boundary lines.
